Global lifestyle apparel leader [Kontoor Brands](https://cxoinsiders.com/kontoor-brands-elects-jamie-caulfield-michael-skipworth/) has officially elected Jamie Caulfield and Michael Skipworth to its board of directors. Adding the seasoned executives expands the company's governing body from seven to nine members. Securing candidates through this Kontoor Brands board election strengthens corporate governance as the parent company of Wrangler, Lee, and Helly Hansen executes long-term consumer growth strategies across its international distribution footprint.

The strategic board expansions bring extensive public company financial oversight, operational scale, and consumer brand management experience to Kontoor Brands. As global retail markets shift, adding deep financial and restaurant industry leadership enhances board-level oversight across supply chain planning, capital allocation, and international franchise expansion.

**Strong Consumer and Financial Executive Track Records**
---------------------------------------------------------

Jamie Caulfield joins the board following a distinguished multi-decade corporate finance career at PepsiCo. Serving most recently as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of PepsiCo Foods North America, Caulfield managed multi-billion-dollar operational budgets, capital deployments, and investor relations strategies. At Kontoor, he will apply his financial acumen as a member of both the Audit Committee and the Nominating & Governance Committee.

Michael Skipworth serves as President, Chief Executive Officer, and Director of Wingstop Inc., overseeing more than 3,000 franchised and company-owned restaurant locations globally. His executive background encompasses diverse financial leadership roles, strategic scaling, and international brand development. Skipworth will leverage his operational management background as a member of Kontoor's Audit Committee and Talent and Compensation Committee.

**Driving Long-Term Growth for Iconic Apparel Portfolios**
----------------------------------------------------------

The board expansion arrives as Kontoor Brands advances strategic investment priorities across its core denim and technical outerwear assets. Enhancing direct-to-consumer digital channels, expanding retail wholesale distribution, and scaling recent brand acquisitions require disciplined capital governance. Integrating C-suite perspectives from leading food, beverage, and franchise operations reinforces the company's commitment to sustained growth.

Scott Baxter, President, Chief Executive Officer, and Chair of the Board at Kontoor Brands, highlighted that welcoming Caulfield and Skipworth strengthens the company's overall strategic posture. He noted that their combined expertise in scaling global consumer brands, navigating complex market environments, and maintaining operational efficiency will prove invaluable as Kontoor drives long-term shareholder value.
