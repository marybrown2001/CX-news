Leading non-profit senior living and care organization [Parker Health Group](https://cxoinsiders.com/parker-health-group-appoints-mark-lenhard-as-next-ceo/) has officially named Mark Lenhard as its incoming President and Chief Executive Officer. Securing top-tier senior living executive leadership through a comprehensive search process positions the historic organization for continued growth across residential care, home health, and community-based healthcare services. Lenhard, a seasoned healthcare executive with three decades of experience in the aging services sector, will officially assume operational leadership in December 2026. He succeeds Roberto Muñiz, who is stepping down after leading Parker Health Group for nearly 28 years.

The high-profile executive transition represents a monumental milestone for the 119-year-old healthcare provider. Marking Parker Health Group’s first chief executive transition in nearly three decades, the appointment ensures strong operational continuity while introducing fresh executive vision. As demographic shifts drive increasing demand for continuum-of-care services across New Jersey and the broader Mid-Atlantic region, Lenhard’s strategic oversight will focus on expanding clinical services, enhancing resident care quality, and integrating advanced digital health technologies across Parker’s long-term care communities.

**Three Decades of Executive Leadership in Aging Services**
-----------------------------------------------------------

Lenhard brings a distinguished background in long-term care administration, non-profit healthcare governance, and multi-site senior housing operations. Most recently serving as President and CEO of United Methodist Communities, he successfully guided complex health systems through changing regulatory landscapes, labor market challenges, and changing post-acute care models. His career highlights include driving operational efficiency, fostering inclusive organizational cultures, and advocating for person-centered care models that enhance the independence and dignity of older adults.

A licensed nursing home administrator with a master’s degree in health administration, Lenhard has spent his career advancing strategic partnerships across public health networks and academic medical centers. His hands-on experience in managing continuing care retirement communities (CCRCs), assisted living facilities, and specialized memory care programs aligns seamlessly with Parker Health Group’s broad operational portfolio.

**Succeeding Roberto Muñiz and Honoring a Legacy of Transformation**
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Roberto Muñiz’s tenure as President and CEO transformed Parker Health Group from a local care provider into one of the nation's most respected aging services organizations. Under his leadership for nearly three decades, Parker expanded its reach across skilled nursing, post-acute rehab, adult day care, and affordable housing options. Muñiz also spearheaded strategic research initiatives and academic partnerships that challenged societal perceptions of aging.

Reflecting on the executive appointment, Parker Health Group’s Board of Trustees commended Muñiz for his decades of visionary service while expressing strong confidence in Lenhard’s ability to guide the organization into its next chapter. The board noted that Lenhard’s proven track record of strategic growth, operational excellence, and mission-driven care makes him uniquely qualified to lead Parker’s dedicated team of healthcare professionals.

**Guiding Strategy for Modern Post-Acute and Senior Living Models**
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Lenhard takes the helm during a transformative era for the broader senior care industry. Modern aging services organizations face evolving regulatory compliance requirements, heightened consumer demand for home-based care options, and an urgent imperative to modernize healthcare IT infrastructure. Under Lenhard’s direction, Parker Health Group will prioritize expanding its community-based support systems, building strategic healthcare alliances, and scaling workforce development initiatives to retain skilled clinical talent.
