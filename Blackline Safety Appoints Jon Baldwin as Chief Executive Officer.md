Connected safety technology leader Blackline Safety has officially announced the appointment of Jon Baldwin as Chief Executive Officer, effective September 28, 2026. Securing experienced executive leadership through this [Blackline Safety CEO appointment](https://cxoinsiders.com/blackline-names-jon-baldwin-ceo/) accelerates the company's global expansion following its recent acquisition by private equity firm Francisco Partners. Baldwin succeeds company founder Cody Slater, who lead the organization for over 15 years and is transitioning into the roles of Board Chair and Chief Architect. The strategic leadership transition reinforces Blackline Safety’s commitment to driving technological innovation across connected gas detection, lone worker monitoring, and industrial safety analytics.

The executive appointment marks a pivotal operational phase for the industrial technology provider. As industrial operations rapidly modernize through internet-of-things (IoT) architectures and real-time cloud analytics, securing scalable leadership ensures Blackline Safety maintains its market trajectory. Baldwin's strategic oversight will focus on accelerating international market growth, scaling hardware-enabled SaaS solutions, and expanding product distribution channels for industrial, commercial, and government safety ecosystems.

**Seasoned Leadership in Connected Hardware and SaaS Platforms**
----------------------------------------------------------------

Baldwin brings more than two decades of senior executive experience leading high-growth technology companies across sensing hardware, connected software platforms, and industrial IoT ecosystems. Most recently, he served as President of Government Solutions at Verra Mobility, where he led intelligent traffic safety platforms and expanded connected hardware networks globally. His career highlights include driving operational scale, optimizing recurring revenue models, and leading multi-disciplinary teams across global manufacturing, engineering, and enterprise sales functions.

His extensive operational background aligns directly with Blackline Safety’s mission to transform workplace safety through real-time connected technologies. By integrating wearable gas detection hardware, satellite connectivity, and cloud-based emergency monitoring, the company helps global industrial organizations safeguard vulnerable workforces and satisfy demanding regulatory standards. Baldwin’s expertise in scaling hardware-backed digital platforms positions the business to capture increasing enterprise demand for unified worker safety solutions.

**Founder Transition to Board Chair and Chief Architect**
---------------------------------------------------------

Cody Slater’s transition to Board Chair and Chief Architect allows Blackline Safety to preserve its core technical vision while gaining fresh executive execution. During his 15-year tenure as CEO, Slater transformed Blackline from an early-stage hardware start-up into a global leader in connected gas detection and lone worker protection. In his new strategic role, Slater will focus directly on long-term technology roadmaps, hardware innovation, and architectural direction for next-generation safety wearables.

Representatives from Francisco Partners commended Slater for building an industry-defining safety platform while welcoming Baldwin as the incoming chief executive. The firm emphasized that combining Baldwin’s operational scaling background with Slater’s technical innovation creates an exceptional leadership dynamic to drive the company's next growth chapter.

**Accelerating Innovation Across Connected Industrial Safety**
--------------------------------------------------------------

Industrial work environments face evolving regulatory compliance rules, heightened operational risk factors, and increasing demand for automated safety workflows. Modern connected safety platforms replace legacy, standalone gas detectors with cloud-connected wearables that provide real-time location tracking, automated fall detection, and instant emergency dispatch capabilities. Under Baldwin’s direction, Blackline Safety will continue expanding its flagship wearable platforms while investing in predictive safety analytics powered by cloud intelligence.
